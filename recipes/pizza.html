Pizza Capricciosa

Pizza Capricciosa is a style of pizza in Italian cuisine prepared with mozzarella cheese, ham, mushrooms (usually champignons), black olives, artichokes, and tomato sauce.

Ingredients

  • Mozzarella cheese
  • Ham
  • Pizza Sauce
  • Olives
  • Mushrooms
  • Artichokes

Preparation Method

  1. Pre-heat the oven to 250°C.
  2. Make your pizza dough of choice then stretch it out into a 30cm.
  3. Slide the rolled base onto the pan (or baking tray) to assemble the pizza on it.
  4. Mix the pizza sauce ingredients in a small bowl.
  5. Sprinkle with cheese first, then scatter/drape/place the toppings all across the surface.
  6. Bake for 10 to 12 minutes or until the crust is crispy and there are light brown spots on the melted cheese
